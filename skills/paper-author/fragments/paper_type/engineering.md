# Engineering and applied papers (petroleum, groundwater, structures, drilling)

- **Open with a specific decision under pressure:** under what operating conditions current practice leads to what consequence.
- **Results answer how the decision changes:** how far a threshold moves, which design wins under which conditions, over what horizon a prediction holds.
- **Separate calibration, independent testing and scenario demonstration** (the ledger should label them). A scenario demonstration is not field validation.
- **Make clear how observables map to model outputs,** so the reader knows whether a number was measured or inferred.
- **State the operating conditions, parameter ranges and data sources next to the methods and results.** Untested extrapolations (across wells, across scenarios) belong in the Discussion as next steps, not implied in the results.
