# Main sections (results, or the body of a theory paper)

- **Each section answers one reader question.** Order the sections by the P5 dependencies, so that a result comes before anything that depends on it.
- **Result first, then the evidence a reader needs to judge it** (a number, an equation, a contrast), then what it means.
- **Method only when the result needs it.** Give the method briefly at the point where understanding the result requires it. Full settings go in the methods section, the appendix or the SI.
- **Let the next paragraph pick up the question the last one left open.** Not every paragraph needs a closing summary.
- **Matching upper and lower bounds, positive and negative results, and treatment and control sit side by side.** Do not make the reader assemble them.
- **After each main theorem, explain the key idea in words:** the construction or the step that makes it work, and why that step is possible. Then say what the result changes.
