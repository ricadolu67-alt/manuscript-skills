# Experimental and observational papers

- **Open with a phenomenon that was directly observed and that existing mechanisms do not explain.** Say what was measured, how, and how large the effect is.
- **Report the observation first, then say what mechanism it supports.** A `MEASUREMENT` is stated directly. A `HYPOTHESIS` is written as "consistent with", alongside the most discriminating control.
- **The decisive control comes before the mechanistic conclusion.** Readers accept an attribution only after they have seen the control.
- **Errors and replicate counts sit next to the numbers,** not collected at the end.
- **State model assumptions, apparatus limits and sample range once, accurately, in the methods.** Restate a limit only when a later conclusion has a different scope. Do not follow every result with "this does not prove a general law".
